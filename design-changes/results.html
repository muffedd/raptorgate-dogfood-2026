How this is ranked

These are the event's current rubric weights:

  • Functionality40%
  • Quality35%
  • Innovation25%

Raw score: each criterion's 1-5 rating multiplied by its weight, then added. The raw mean is the average of a project's valid weighted scores.

Adjusted score: for each judge's valid reviews of eligible projects, calculate their raw mean and population standard deviation. Each raw review is adjusted to clamp(3 + (raw - judge mean) / judge standard deviation, 1, 5). A judge with zero spread contributes 3.00 instead. The normalized project mean averages those adjusted reviews.

Rank by the normalized mean rounded to three decimals, highest first. Equal three-decimal means share a competition rank; project slug breaks display-order ties. No minimum review count or cross-judge overlap is required. Sparse or disjoint assignments may distort the adjusted order; normalization is a calculation, not proof of statistical fairness. Individual ballots and judge identities remain private.
