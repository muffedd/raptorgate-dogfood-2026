Submission questions

Up to ten questions. Each needs a lowercase key, a label and an optional required flag.

  1. KeyLowercase, unique, e.g. stack. Locked after the first submission.
  2. LabelThe question teams see. Fix wording, not meaning.
  3. RequiredOptional flag. Locked after the first submission.

After a submission exists, question keys and required flags cannot change. Labels may be corrected, but changing their meaning would mislabel earlier answers.
