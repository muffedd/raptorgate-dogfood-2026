What each check means

Certificate

Ed25519 signature and this site's published issuance. For independent issuer trust, compare the key with one published by the organizer through a trusted channel. Run verify.py offline to check the signature without this site.

Receipt

Matches an accepted ballot after results are published. It does not expose the voter.

Record

Matches this server's private HMAC key after publication. A record is not independently verifiable without that private key.

None of these checks proves judging quality, that a person cast a vote, or overall fairness.
